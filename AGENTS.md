# Public-core workspace instructions

Read `GITHUB_WORKSPACE.md` first and follow the linked private companion project
instructions and contracts. Preserve its business, media, content, publication
and multi-machine work boundaries.

For a new identity, substantial redesign or extending an established brand
across requested page families, apply [the website design pipeline](docs/WEBSITE_DESIGN_PIPELINE.md)
and [website-design-system](skills/website-design-system/SKILL.md). Plan the
authorized route/journey, page purposes, responsive structure, shared design
decisions, states and asset reuse before detailed section production. Preserve
the active site's chosen identity while adapting marketing, shop, contact,
auth and dashboard layouts to their actual tasks.

For ImageGen-led section design or deriving a selected mockup into production
assets, apply [the section asset-kit rule](docs/WEBPAGE_SECTION_ASSET_KITS.md)
and [the reusable skill](skills/webpage-section-asset-kit/SKILL.md). New or
substantially redesigned image-led sections use this workflow when it benefits
their brief; small text/CSS fixes and vector-only tasks do not require ImageGen.
This supplements the companion builder/planner/audit/impeccable workflows.

Keep each site's art direction distinct. Use the existing media pipeline; do
not create a per-site optimizer. Keep text and controls editable and verify the
actual rendered composition, not only individual images. Ordinary delegated
design decisions do not acquire a new owner-approval gate from this rule.
Review section junctions and the full set of requested pages, with relevant
loading/empty/error/success states and real next actions. Not every section
needs ImageGen, and no niche inherits another site's palette or layout.

During authorized builds, record demonstrated reusable gaps and apply the
[builder-led improvement loop](skills/website-design-system/references/improvement-loop.md).
Follow the companion's CORE_IMPROVEMENT contract when present. Small authorized,
coordinated rule/skill/helper repairs are done by the builder and verified;
Git/PR/adoption status stays explicit, with no mandatory artificial changes.

For an authorized launch, production update, hosting migration or deployment
runbook, apply [website-deploy](skills/website-deploy/SKILL.md). Preserve existing
DNS/mail/data and shared publication; verify the public host and distinguish
build, upload, activation and delivery. Local builds or writing instructions
alone do not authorize live changes.

This rule authorizes no deployment, commerce, external messaging, merge or
native-app changes. Follow actual user scope and the coordination contracts.
