# Page families

Read only the families needed by the active request. These are decision criteria, not mandatory templates or authorization to add business modules. Choose different niche identities; within one site extend its approved identity into task-appropriate layouts.

## Homepage and marketing pages

Build a coherent reading-to-action sequence. Explain the offer, useful outcome, mechanism, available evidence, choices and next step in the order this audience needs. A technical product may need an interactive explanation; a local service may need actual process/coverage; an editorial product may need a scannable index. Section counts and layouts follow those needs.

Use an expressive representative visual where it helps identity. Supporting sections should contribute new information. Repeated cards are appropriate for parallel choices, not as a compulsory container for all copy. Reviews and trust evidence require real sources; isolated design samples remain identified as examples and never become ratings/schema or manufactured proof.

Plan contextual actions and transitions along the whole page. Footer/nav should expose useful real paths. Animation must preserve reading, action and reduced-motion use; prefer restrained motion linked to orientation or feedback over constant ambient activity.

## Shop, category and product choice

For an authorized production payment/order integration, read [commerce acceptance](commerce-acceptance.md). It supplements visual selection with seller/provider readiness, actual order delivery, real-review provenance and distinct local/provider/live evidence. An enquiry-only shop does not need a payment integration.

Prioritize selection: what is included, meaningful variants, real price/availability when supplied, compatibility/limits, quantities and actual next step. Adapt layout to a small curated set, technical catalogue, service options or another real offering. Filters need to distinguish the actual collection; omit purposeless search/filter controls for a handful of bundles.

Reuse approved product masters and match camera, scale and illumination. A quantity arrangement can repeat one faithful object. Use live text for package contents, labels, selected options and prices; selected states change the relevant surface/outline and retain semantic selection.

Plan mobile comparison, option changing, summary visibility, invalid combinations and empty/no-match states when relevant. Connect offer choice → detail/configuration → basket/enquiry/next step only through the actual scoped capabilities. A physical-looking software box is a marketing illustration, not evidence of a shipped box. Distinguish software delivery from hardware fulfilment when applicable.

Missing inventory, currency, tax/shipping or payment facts remain dependencies; do not invent a live shop to fill a visual plan. An explicitly requested private future-shop prototype may use isolated fixtures and a clear demo boundary. Production payment/order behavior requires its own actual integration and acceptance; the visual skill neither enables it nor imposes a blanket prohibition on later authorized commerce.

## Contacts and enquiry

Design around the reason to contact and the actual supported channels. Use active-site identity/contacts only. A local service may need verified coverage and a real location; a software product may need support category/context and an email route. A map, phone or response-time promise belongs only when supported by real facts.

Keep the form proportional to the task, with visible labels, useful instructions and retained values after recoverable errors. Plan pending/validation/backend-error/success states and a useful fallback. Success follows the actual durable submission outcome; stored enquiry and delivered email remain separate results. Sending a form must not silently create marketing consent.

Use quiet brand accents and appropriate trust/privacy context. A decorative contact image is optional. Do not force ImageGen into a page whose quality comes from typography, spacing and a usable form. Reference: [W3C form guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/).

## Login, registration and recovery

Preserve the brand while keeping the form and next action dominant. Provide actual login/register/recovery relationships, clear labels and validation/recovery instructions; include only fields necessary for the supported account model. Decorative side art is optional and may disappear on narrow screens.

Use appropriate browser autofill semantics such as username/current-password/new-password, permit password-manager/paste use and make visibility controls understandable. Keep focus, errors and pending states clear. Reference: [MDN autocomplete](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/autocomplete).

Align verification, session-expiry and redirect behavior with the existing auth API. Frontend validation and hidden links do not establish authentication or server authorization. Do not implement a fake local-password store, leak tokens or claim that a demo login creates an account. For an explicitly private frontend task isolate sample states and document the missing backend, rather than presenting a production success.

## Creator, customer or admin dashboard

Start with actual roles and recurring jobs: what must the user notice, create, edit, monitor or resolve? Map task priority, information/data source, permitted actions and drill-down paths. A creator dashboard is not automatically a wall of revenue cards; invent neither payouts/earnings nor analytics to decorate it.

Use a persistent navigation and information density appropriate to the tasks. Share brand typography, restrained accents and component semantics with the public site; allow calmer surfaces for sustained operation. Keep filters, tables/lists, contextual actions and details visually legible. Charts need meaningful units/time ranges and a useful data/empty alternative.

Plan long labels, large amounts, loading/empty/error/success, first-use guidance, role-denied/session-expired states and save/retry/unsaved-change handling where the task requires them. Mobile can prioritize tasks and use expandable details or accessible horizontal data regions; do not squeeze a desktop grid until every label becomes unreadable.

Respect existing mutation and permission APIs. Do not duplicate auth, payments or another business module through this visual workflow. Demo records stay isolated, labelled and outside real operational measurements.

## Editorial, help and legal pages

Retain the identity while prioritizing reading/navigation. Use meaningful headings, readable line lengths, contextual illustrations/diagrams when useful, actual sources/attribution and genuine next steps. Legal content is not invented from a design reference. Quiet typography can be the complete visual solution; a generated hero is not mandatory.
