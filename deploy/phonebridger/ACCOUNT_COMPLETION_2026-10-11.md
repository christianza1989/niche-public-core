## Published behavior

Canonical Worker `3f2e3fd0-f788-4ab4-ad3d-45d81087d76c` and playground `b1c8cf5c-93cf-4ba2-89fc-99d02e085de1` serve the final source. Canonical account settings request genuine verification mail; `/verify` requires explicit single-use confirmation. `/recover` provides genuine reset mail and password confirmation. Tokens travel in fragments, are immediately removed from browser history, are stored only as hashes and expire. Successful reset revokes all sessions and sibling reset tokens. The existing scrypt parameters and site-specific six-character minimum are preserved; other shared-core sites retain their defaults.

Support tickets retain their idempotent saved reference. Staff notifications and verified, explicitly opted-in customer reply notifications use the existing owned Hostinger service mailbox. Outbox payloads are encrypted with AES-GCM and cleared after send. Atomic claims permit at most one send attempt; ambiguous/interrupted delivery is never automatically replayed. Daily cleanup expires tokens and outbox records. Deleting an account removes verification/reset tokens, expires queued mail and suspends its creator capability without deleting financial audit records.

The human approved Stripe recipient onboarding with MB Memocasting paying provider fees and accepting recipient negative-balance responsibility. Accounts v2 creates a recipient-only Express account from the authenticated approved creator, after verified email/current terms and country selection. Stripe hosts identity/bank onboarding. Website return URLs never imply verified status. The initial supported country is LT, matching the actual merchant; adding countries requires a supported provider configuration rather than guessing regional availability.

Daily cron at 03:17 UTC performs reconciliation and, from the 15th, monthly eligible USD25+ commission batches. Merchant identity/payout capability, recipient metadata/environment/country/capabilities, approved creator/current terms and verified email are checked. One bounded batch per creator/month holds at most 20 commission records; further balances carry to the next month. This initial capacity is explicit and should be revisited before high-volume onboarding. Unknown transfers stay reserved and are read-only reconciled by exact provider transfer group; they are never blindly resent. Only canonical Stripe transfer confirmation credits Paid. Refunds before/during/after transfer reconcile cumulative proportional reversals without negative buckets. Paid means credited to the recipient Stripe balance; bank arrival is not represented.
