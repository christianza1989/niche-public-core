# Payment brand artwork

Unmodified SVG brand artwork retrieved2026-10-27 from the community SVG collection https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos : logos/visa.svg, logos/mastercard.svg and logos/stripe.svg. The trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Use here identifies card brands and the existing Stripe payment processor; it is not an endorsement or certification badge.

Stripe wordmark colour guidance: https://stripe.com/newsroom/information . No other payment methods are advertised. Rendering is conditioned on the existing storefront's admitted live payment availability. SVGs were inspected to contain no scripts, foreignObject, event handlers, embedded styles, image references or external hrefs.
